#Intro
Thirty-nine years... Whis. The dream again. Find me that Saiyan.
#Verse 1
Thirty-nine years asleep; one blink behind my eyes,
While empires turned to embers in the corners of the skies.
No cradle, no confession, just the office and the blade;
I am what the light requires when its monuments decay.
The Kais may seed the heavens; I decide what cannot stay—
A garden chokes on everything its keeper fears to slay.
Yet through my sacred silence came a flash of burning red:
A stranger crowned in godfire, standing where my dreams lay dead.

#Bridge
A little thread named Shin can bring a god down to his knees;
Even cosmic kings have chains that no one ever sees.
And above me, Zeno laughs—such a small and sovereign sound;
Tell me, what is DIVINITY when every god is bound?
I was made to clear the garden, not to grieve for every rose;
Still that crimson ghost inside my dreams refuses to let go.
If he rises, I will meet him. If he fails, a world may fall—
But if he makes me HESITATE... perhaps that says it ALL!
